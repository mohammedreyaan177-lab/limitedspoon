## Intent and cannibalization rules

- Keep `/` for the overall practice and broad branded/commercial intent.
- Keep `/care` as the single hub for seven verified care areas; add dedicated service pages only when distinct facts and practitioner-reviewed content can support them.
- Let `/approach` explain process and personalization, not diagnoses.
- Keep `/about` for verified entity/founder details and `/contact` for enquiries.
- Do not create city pages, “near me” content, online-service claims, or LocalBusiness markup until the corresponding service facts are confirmed.
- Health content must be reviewed by the practice and should not imply nutrition support diagnoses, treats, cures, or replaces clinical care.
