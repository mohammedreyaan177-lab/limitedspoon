# SEO keyword map

## Research basis and limits

This map is based on the practice's verified services and existing English copy. It groups queries by likely intent; it does not claim search volume, competition, or ranking difficulty. Google Search Console, Keyword Planner, and Trends data were not available. Geography is unconfirmed, so no city or “near me” targeting is assigned. Validate these themes against Search Console after launch before expanding content.

## Branded and question-led themes

- **Branded:** Limited Spoon Wellness; Limited Spoon nutrition; Shalini Selvakumar nutritionist.
- **Question-led (future content only if the practice wants an editorial section):** How can nutrition guidance fit an ordinary routine? What should I ask before changing my diet while managing a medical condition? What does personalised nutrition support involve?
- **Service-specific commercial:** nutrition support for PCOS; nutrition guidance for diabetes; nutrition during pregnancy and after birth; nutrition support for older adults; nutrition alongside heart and lung care.
- **Broad informational:** balanced meals; nutrition label literacy; practical food habits. Homepage already contains introductory education, but a blog or resource hub does not exist.

Health-related content should be reviewed and attributed by the practice before publication. Avoid implying that nutrition advice treats, cures, or replaces clinical care; the current site appropriately states that care is complementary and not medical advice.

## Cannibalization guardrails

- Keep the home page for the overall practice and broad personalised nutrition intent.
- Keep `/care` as the single hub for all seven areas; use its section anchors rather than seven near-duplicate pages.
- Let `/approach` own how the service works, not the list of diagnoses.
- Reserve `/about` for entity and founder information; reserve `/contact` for enquiry intent.
- Create individual service pages only when there is enough verified, distinct, clinically reviewed content to answer a separate search need.
